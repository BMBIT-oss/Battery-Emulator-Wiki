## Compatible batteries

This platform has a 20kWh battery.

- Nissan Sakura 2022–present
- Mitsubishi eK X

![image](../images/nissan-sakura-mitsubishi-ek-x-ev-01.png)

### Special considerations

- Requires external [GPIO contactor control](nissan_leaf_e_nv200.md/#automatic-control)

## Software configuration

For this battery type, use the option called [Nissan LEAF](nissan_leaf_e_nv200.md) under the "Battery Protocol" section.

## Part numbers 

The LV connectors are [the same as on Nissan LEAF](nissan_leaf_e_nv200.md/#part-numbers-for-nissan-leaf-batteries).

## Wiring, Low voltage connector

The Low Voltage connector is the same 36pin battery connector (2013-2023) Yazaki 7287-1065-30 used on the Nissan LEAF.
